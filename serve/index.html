How it decides

PatchCore never sees a defect. It remembers what thousands of small patches of good parts look like, and flags any patch that is far from all of them. The heatmap is that distance.

The CNN is a ResNet-18 trained on good and defective examples. Its heatmap is Grad-CAM: the parts of the image that pushed it towards "defect".

The threshold was chosen per part type on a calibration set, and the scores above were measured on separate holdout images. Both models run here on ONNX Runtime, on the CPU.
